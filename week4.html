// EXPOSURE LOG

One of the homework activities this week was 'Slave to the Algorithm', a challenge where we would let a coin flip determine our choices throughout the day. As someone who struggles with indecisivenesss, I actually already do something similar to slave to the algorithm in my daily life: I would always assign two tasks to some random numbers and then ask my friends to choose between those numbers. I think by asking such questions, there's also a type of psychology behind it as it also helps me realise which option i truly want in the moment when im being indecisive.
