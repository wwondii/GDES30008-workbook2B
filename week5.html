// EXPOSURE LOG

Week 5's activity was to create a self-portrait or portrait of a loved one. I chose to make a portrait of a cat I helped take care of during the holidays called Ponyo. Creating this portrait was actually a lot more challenging than I thought as I struggled a lot with the x and y placements of the shapes and lines. In the end, I then experimented with Claude, asking it to mirror the x and y coordinates I already had so I could make the portrait feel more alive. I then went through the github resources for our class and made the addition of the polkadot background and cursor tracking feature to make the portrait feel more alive.
